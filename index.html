What is your company going to make?

Senior Fraud Alert is an app downloaded onto a senior's phone (by a grandchild or child) that targets the scam epidemic faced by seniors. It has two major features:
1. Scam Calls: We block scam calls before they ring, and we do it in a way that is better than existing solutions. Most scam blocking apps use a block list (a list of phone numbers known to be spam), but these lists are outdated. Instead, we maintain our own block list from the reports of all users, and we also use the metadata of the incoming call to block scammers in real-time, without relying on their phone number. It works well, and we've tested it against the most common scam calls.
2. Scam Texts: We use a local LLM that can read your texts on-device to detect scams with over 95% accuracy, and then we filter those messages away. And it all happens on-device, so your messages stay private.
If we can get this into the hands of every senior in America, we can reduce senior fraud to almost zero.
