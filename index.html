What is your company going to make? Please describe your product and what it does or will do.

Senior Fraud Alert is an app downloaded onto a senior's phone (by a grandchild or child) that targets senior scams through monitoring texts, calls, and websites. Texts not from contacts are put through elder fraud pattern checkers and, if fraudulent, go to junk with a logged reason. For calls, we feed iOS our own dynamically growing fraud-number list. Known scammers are silenced, or may ring as “Possible Medicare Scam” instead of a generic “Spam.” For websites, a senior can paste in any text they're unsure about, and the app scans it. In the future, we want website monitoring so no more copy-pasting. Even further steps would be family alerts, one-tap calling to a trusted contact, and Gmail and Outlook integrations.

Idea

Why did you pick this idea to work on? Do you have domain expertise in this area? How do you know people need what you're making?

My 86-year-old Grandma was getting targeted far more than I was. Because I worked in cybersecurity at Netflix for behavioral remediation and vulnerability management, it was very obvious what techniques could be used specifically for older users. To scale it, I brought on Kachi, who ran traffic engineering for 300M+ users at Netflix.
Beyond my grandma and the stories I've heard in retirement homes, the FBI data shows seniors reported $7.75B in fraud losses, up 59% year over year, with the FTC estimating true losses at $119B.

Who are your competitors? What do you understand about your business that they don't?

We understand that cybersecurity requires knowing your audience and being continually preventative rather than reactionary. Call and text screeners like Robokiller, Savi, and Scammer Guardian focus on the call itself. Once a scammer gets through and convinces someone, there's no further monitoring to stop the money from moving. Behavioral helpers like ScamPrep rely on training, which is ineffective, as most seniors already know they're targets. They need a control that steps in at the moment of panic. Looking at larger competitors, Apple and Google build for everyone, so they play it safe: few false alarms, nothing shared with family. We can be aggressive, focus on the scams seniors actually face, and loop in family the moment something's off. Financial monitors like EverSafe, Carefull, and Aura notify family after the money moves. We stop the scam before this happens.

How do or will you make money? How much could you make?

Seniors don't want to download the app, but their kids and grandkids absolutely do, so we sell to families at $25/month to prevent an average loss of $38,500. We started with senior living communities where one staff partnership reaches every family in the building at once. Since there are about 1.5M seniors in assisted and independent living, converting 10% of their families could net us 150K subscribers, or $45M ARR. Next, we will expand to the 51M Americans caring for an aging parent at home, then eventually sell to banks and credit unions that eat the losses when scams succeed.
